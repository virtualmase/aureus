02 / Design principles

Trust is an output of the system, not a promise on the website.

01

Bound autonomy

Mandates, spend limits, targets, and environments are machine-readable constraints—not buried operational guidance.

02

Make evidence portable

Proof certificates turn decisions into durable artifacts that can move across operators, auditors, and systems.

03

Fail safe by default

Containment begins in dry-run mode. Enforcement follows only after policy, telemetry, and rollback paths are understood.

04

Separate authority from execution

An agent can propose an action without inheriting unlimited permission to take it.
