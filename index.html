The Origins: From Nāranga to AUREUS

The name **AUREUS** embodies a profound philosophy, tracing its roots to the ancient concept of the "golden orange tree." The word **'Nāranga'**, meaning "orange tree," was influenced by a folk etymology connecting it with **'aurum'** – Latin for 'gold'.

This rich lineage informs our vision: like a golden orange tree, AUREUS is a resilient, living system that continuously cultivates and bears fruit—intelligence, wisdom, and flourishing. It is a symbol of abundance, interconnectedness, and the enduring cycle of growth, much like the sovereign intellect we are building.

We believe in a future where technology is a force for harmonious growth, yielding golden outcomes for humanity and the planet.
